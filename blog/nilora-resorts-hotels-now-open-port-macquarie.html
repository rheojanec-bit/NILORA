NILORA Resorts & Hotels Is Now Open in Port Macquarie

10 September 2026 4 min read NILORA Resorts & Hotels
Home › Explore › NILORA Is Now Open

A new coastal escape has arrived in Port Macquarie. NILORA Resorts & Hotels is now open, welcoming families, couples, friends and groups to experience spacious villa living, landscaped surroundings and resort-style facilities in a relaxed coastal setting.

Designed as more than simply somewhere to sleep, NILORA gives guests room to slow down, reconnect and enjoy Port Macquarie at their own pace.

NILORA Resorts & Hotels, 288 Hastings River Drive, Port Macquarie NSW 2444.

A New Way to Stay in Port Macquarie

At NILORA, the freedom and comfort of self-contained villa accommodation come together with the experience of a resort.

Spend the day discovering Port Macquarie, enjoy time around the resort, stay active or simply settle into your villa for a slower afternoon. With generous spaces and a welcoming atmosphere, your stay can be as relaxed or as active as you choose.

It's about making the resort part of your holiday — not just somewhere to sleep.

It's about making the resort part of your holiday — not just somewhere to sleep.

Space to Stay Your Way

Every getaway is different, which is why NILORA offers a selection of villas designed for different ways of travelling.

Couples can retreat to the One Bedroom Garden Villa, while small families and friends can enjoy the flexibility of the Two Bedroom Garden Villa. For larger families and groups, the Three Bedroom Garden Villa provides generous shared living, while the Three Bedroom Lagoon Villa offers a signature waterside setting for those looking for something extra special.

Whether it's a weekend for two, a family holiday or time away with friends, NILORA gives you space to stay your way.

Spacious, self-contained villas for couples, families and groups.

More Than Somewhere to Sleep

A memorable getaway is about more than your accommodation. Across NILORA, guests can enjoy:

  • Landscaped resort grounds
  • Indoor pool
  • Wellness & sauna facilities
  • Fitness facilities
  • Tennis
  • Games & recreation areas
  • BBQ & outdoor gathering spaces
  • Convenient onsite parking

Start the day with something active, spend time together around the resort or simply slow down and enjoy having nowhere else you need to be.

Resort facilities including the indoor pool, open daily 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM.

Your Port Macquarie Escape

Beyond the resort, Port Macquarie and its coastal surroundings are waiting to be discovered. Explore during the day and return to the space and comfort of NILORA when you're ready to unwind.

It's a stay designed around choice — explore when you want to explore, relax when you want to relax.

Your Coastal Escape Awaits

With our doors now open, we're excited to welcome you to NILORA Resorts & Hotels and a new resort experience in Port Macquarie.

Come for a weekend away, bring the family together, gather with friends or simply give yourself a few days to slow down.

Relax. Play. Reconnect.

Discover NILORA, explore our villas and start planning your Port Macquarie escape today.
